## Legal Bases for Processing

We only process your and your child's personal information where we have a lawful basis to do so. Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Article 6, the bases we rely on are:

- **Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)):** to create and manage your Account, to provide the Products and their core tracking features, to process your purchases, and to provide customer support.
- **Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)):** to process delicate (health-adjacent) information about your child as described below, to process photos and attachments you choose to add, to use the Starlie AI assistant features that process such information, and to send you promotional messages. You can withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.
- **Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)):** to secure your Account and our Products, to prevent fraud and abuse, to understand and improve how the Products are used, and to create and use Anonymous Information. Where we rely on legitimate interests, we balance them against your rights and interests.
- **Compliance with a legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)):** to meet our legal, tax, and regulatory obligations, and to respond to lawful requests from authorities.

For users in Ukraine, we process personal data on one of the grounds set out in Article 11 of the Law of Ukraine "On Personal Data Protection" (No. 2297-VI), which provides an equivalent set of grounds, including the data subject's consent, the performance of a contract to which the data subject is a party, and the legitimate interests of the controller.
