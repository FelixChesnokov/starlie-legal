# Starlie — Support

**Starlie** is a baby tracker for parents of children aged 0–3: sleep, feeding, diapers, walks, pumping, solids, growth measurements, a diary, developmental leaps, and an AI helper.

---

## Contact us

**Email:** support.starlie@gmail.com

We reply within **2 business days**. Support is available in **English, Ukrainian, and Polish**.

To help us resolve your issue faster, please include:

- the email address of your Starlie account;
- your device model and iOS version (Settings → General → About);
- the app version (Starlie → Settings, at the bottom of the screen);
- a short description of what happened and what you expected;
- a screenshot, if the problem is visible on screen.

## Frequently asked questions

### Subscription

**What does the Pro subscription include?**
Growth leaps for ages 0–36 months, the AI helper, personalized daily tips, extended statistics, and the full solids journal with allergen tracking.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside Starlie:

1. Open **Settings** on your iPhone.
2. Tap your name at the top, then **Subscriptions**.
3. Select **Starlie** and tap **Cancel Subscription**.

Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep Pro access until the end of the period you already paid for.

**How do I cancel the free trial?**
The same way. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, otherwise Apple charges for the first period automatically.

**I subscribed but Pro features are locked.**
Open **Starlie → Settings → Subscription → Restore Purchases**. If that does not help, write to us and include the email address of your Starlie account.

**I reinstalled the app / switched to a new iPhone. How do I get my subscription back?**
Sign in with the same account, then use **Settings → Subscription → Restore Purchases**. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID that made the purchase.

**How do I request a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Go to **reportaproblem.apple.com**, sign in with your Apple ID, find Starlie, and request a refund.

### Account and data

**How do I delete my account?**
**Starlie → Settings → Delete Account.** This permanently removes your account and all associated data, including children's profiles, tracking records, diary entries, and photos. The action cannot be undone.

Deleting the account does **not** cancel an active subscription — cancel it separately in your iPhone Settings (see above).

**How do I get a copy of my data, or exercise other privacy rights?**
Write to **support.starlie@gmail.com** with the email address of your Starlie account.

**Can my partner or grandparents use the same data?**
Yes. Starlie supports family access: invite them by email from the app, and they will see the same child's data. Invitations are private — only the person you invite by email can join.

### App

**Which languages does Starlie support?**
English, Ukrainian, Polish, and Russian. Change it in **Settings → Language**.

**I am not receiving notifications.**
Check that notifications are enabled both in **iPhone Settings → Starlie → Notifications** and in **Starlie → Settings → Notifications**.

**Does Starlie give medical advice?**
No. Starlie provides general informational and educational content about childcare only. It is not a medical service, not a medical device, and not a substitute for professional medical advice. For any concern about your child's health, contact a pediatrician. See our [Disclaimer](starlie-disclaimer.md).
